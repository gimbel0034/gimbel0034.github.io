United States Marine Corps

The United States Marine Corps is known for readiness, discipline, courage, and commitment to national defense. Marines are trained to respond rapidly during conflicts, humanitarian missions, and emergency situations.

Core Values
  • Honor
  • Courage
  • Commitment

United States Military

The United States Military includes the Army, Navy, Marine Corps, Air Force, Coast Guard, and Space Force. These branches serve to protect the nation, support allies, and provide assistance during natural disasters and humanitarian crises.

  • Leadership
  • Teamwork
  • Preparedness
  • Service Before Self
  • National Defense

Police Officers

Police officers serve communities by protecting public safety, enforcing laws, assisting citizens, and promoting justice. Their work requires integrity, fairness, communication, and responsibility.

  • Public Safety
  • Justice
  • Community Service
  • Protection of Rights
  • Ethical Leadership
