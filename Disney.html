🍽 Disney Recipe Kingdom

🐭 Mickey Pancakes

Ingredients

  • 2 Cups Pancake Mix
  • 1 Egg
  • 1 Cup Milk
  • Maple Syrup
  • Fresh Fruit

Directions

  1. Mix ingredients together.
  2. Create one large Mickey face and two ears.
  3. Cook until golden brown.
  4. Serve with berries and syrup.

🍍 Disney Dole Whip

Ingredients

  • Frozen Pineapple
  • Vanilla Ice Cream
  • Pineapple Juice

Directions

  1. Blend until smooth.
  2. Pipe into serving glass.
  3. Freeze for 10 minutes.
  4. Enjoy your Disney snack.
