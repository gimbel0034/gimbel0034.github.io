🍽 Disney Recipes

Mickey Pancakes

Ingredients

  • 2 cups pancake mix
  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup milk

Directions

  1. Mix ingredients.
  2. Create one large circle and two small circles.
  3. Cook until golden brown.
  4. Serve with strawberries and syrup.

Disney Dole Whip

Ingredients

  • 2 cups frozen pineapple
  • 1 scoop vanilla ice cream
  • 1/4 cup pineapple juice

Directions

  1. Blend all ingredients.
  2. Pipe into serving bowl.
  3. Enjoy a Disney favorite.
