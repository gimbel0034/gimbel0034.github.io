What the Woods Teach Us

The forest is a patient teacher. Every branch, leaf, and shadow holds a lesson.

  • Patience: Trees grow slowly, but they never stop reaching for the light.
  • Resilience: Storms bend branches, but roots hold firm.
  • Cycles: Leaves fall, snow melts, and life returns.
  • Stillness: Silence is not emptiness—it’s space to listen.
