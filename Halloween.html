Jack‑O’‑Lantern Orange Juice Potion

Bright, citrusy, kid‑friendly, and perfect with candy.

  • 2 cups chilled orange juice
  • 1/2 cup sparkling water or lemon‑lime soda
  • 1 tbsp honey or simple syrup (optional)
  • Ice cubes shaped like tiny pumpkins (or regular ice)
  • Orange slice + cinnamon stick for garnish

Stir orange juice with honey until smooth. Fill a glass with pumpkin ice cubes, pour in the juice, then top with sparkling water. Garnish with an orange slice and cinnamon stick. Serve in a clear cup with a drawn‑on jack‑o’‑lantern face for extra Halloween charm.

Purple Witches’ Brew (Non‑Alcoholic)

Magical, fruity, and glowing under blacklight.

  • 1 cup grape juice (100% juice)
  • 1/2 cup blueberry juice or puree
  • 1/2 cup lemon‑lime soda or sparkling water
  • Handful of frozen blueberries (“floating eyeballs”)
  • Optional: edible glitter or shimmer dust

In a cauldron‑style bowl or large glass, mix grape and blueberry juice. Add frozen blueberries so they bob like spooky eyes. Top with soda for fizz, then sprinkle a tiny pinch of edible glitter. Serve with a striped straw and call it your Purple Witches Brew.

Learning Concept Art with Color

Try a mini concept‑art exercise: design a Halloween scene that mixes jack‑o’‑lantern orange, flag red‑white‑blue, and witchy purple. Add a pink puppy and a purple dinosaur as characters. Ask yourself: “Which color feels spooky, which feels silly, and which feels safe?” That’s the start of learning color psychology in art.
