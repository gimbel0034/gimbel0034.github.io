🎃 Fresh Garden Pumpkin Pie 🎃

Made from Real Fresh Sugar Pumpkins Straight from the Garden

🌱 Fresh Pumpkin Ingredients

  • 1 Fresh Sugar Pumpkin
  • 3 Eggs
  • 1 Cup Heavy Cream
  • 2/3 Cup Brown Sugar
  • 1 tsp Cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp Ginger
  • 1/4 tsp Nutmeg
  • 1 tsp Vanilla Extract
  • Homemade Pie Crust

🥧 Directions

  1. Pick a fresh sugar pumpkin from the garden.
  2. Roast at 375°F for 45-60 minutes.
  3. Scoop out the soft pumpkin flesh.
  4. Blend into a smooth puree.
  5. Mix pumpkin with eggs, cream, sugar, and spices.
  6. Pour into pie crust.
  7. Bake 15 minutes at 425°F.
  8. Reduce to 350°F and bake 45 minutes.
  9. Cool completely before serving.
