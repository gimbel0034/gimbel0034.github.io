About Mental Health

Mental health includes emotional, psychological, and social well-being. It affects how people think, feel, act, manage stress, relate to others, and make healthy choices. Mental health is an important part of overall health through every stage of life.

Brief History of Mental Health

Throughout history, mental health conditions were often misunderstood. During ancient times many societies believed mental illness was connected to spiritual or supernatural causes. Medical understanding improved during the 18th, 19th, and 20th centuries as psychology, psychiatry, and neuroscience advanced. Today, healthcare professionals recognize mental health conditions as real medical conditions that can often be treated with therapy, education, support systems, lifestyle changes, and medications when appropriate.
