🎃 Farm Fresh Pumpkin Pie 🎃

Garden to Table Complete Processing Project

🌱 Step 1: Harvest Your Pumpkin

Choose a ripe sugar pumpkin from the garden.
Wash thoroughly under cool running water.
Scrub away dirt and garden debris.

🔪 Step 2: Clean Pumpkin

Cut off stem.
Slice pumpkin in half.
Scoop out seeds.
Remove stringy fibers.

🔥 Step 3: Roast Pumpkin

Preheat oven to 375°F.
Place pumpkin halves face down.
Bake 45–60 minutes.
Cool completely.

🥄 Step 4: Create Pumpkin Puree

Scoop out soft flesh.
Blend until silky smooth.
Drain excess moisture.
Measure 2 cups puree.

🥧 Pie Ingredients

  • 2 cups fresh pumpkin puree
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 3/4 cup brown sugar
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp ginger
  • 1/4 tsp nutmeg
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • Homemade pie crust

🥄 Make Filling

  • Mix pumpkin puree
  • Add eggs
  • Add cream
  • Add spices
  • Whisk until smooth
  • Pour into crust

🔥 Bake Pie

Bake 15 minutes at 425°F.
Reduce to 350°F.
Bake another 45 minutes.
Cool completely.
Ready To Bake
