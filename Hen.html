Safety Plans with Maps

A safety plan can live on a map: where to meet, what to bring, and how to stay safe during activities.

  • Choose a safe meeting spot in Henderson (park, community center).
  • Mark “water stations” and shaded areas on your map.
  • Plan routes for walks or bike rides with safe crossings.
  • For Utah trips, mark trailheads and rest points.
  • Use a Google‑style map to visualize emergency contacts and locations.
  • Write a simple checklist: water, sun protection, phone, buddy system.
