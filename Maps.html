• Create picture frames for Utah and Nevada landscapes.
• Use circles, rectangles, and lines to draw math shapes and art at the same time.
• Build a digital gallery with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

Learning About Life & Safety Plan

Life, art, maps, and math all connect. You can use them to plan safe, creative days and understand the world step by step.

  • Plan activities with safety first: water, gear, rest.
  • Use maps to understand where you are and where you’re going.
  • Use math to count time, money, and distances.
  • Use art to express feelings about life situations.
  • Use circles and frames to organize ideas visually.
  • Write a simple safety plan for sports, trips, and games.
