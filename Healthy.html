🏥 Healthcare Awareness

Good healthcare includes physical wellness, emotional wellness, proper nutrition, exercise, sleep, and access to medical support. Regular checkups and healthy lifestyles can help people enjoy a higher quality of life.

🧠 Mental Health Information

  • Schizoaffective Disorder combines symptoms of schizophrenia and mood disorders.
  • Bipolar Disorder involves significant mood episodes including depression and mania or hypomania.
  • Anxiety Disorders may involve excessive worry, fear, panic attacks, or social anxiety.
  • Obsessive Compulsive Disorder (OCD) involves intrusive thoughts and repetitive behaviors.
  • Eating Disorders affect eating habits, body image, and overall health.

Mental health treatment may involve therapy, support systems, healthy coping skills, medication when prescribed, and professional care. If someone is struggling, seeking help from qualified healthcare professionals is important.
