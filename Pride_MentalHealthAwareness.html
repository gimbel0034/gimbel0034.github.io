Pride, Freedom & Mental Health Awareness

LGBTQ+ Pride

This space honors LGBTQ+ identities and experiences. Pride is about visibility, safety, and the freedom to live authentically.

#LoveIsLove #Equality #Pride

Learning about Autism

Autism is a neurodevelopmental difference that affects how someone experiences communication, social interaction, and sensory input. Understanding autism means respecting different ways of thinking and being.

#Neurodiversity #Acceptance

Bipolar Disorder & Schizoaffective

Bipolar disorder involves shifts in mood, energy, and activity levels. Schizoaffective disorder includes mood symptoms along with features like hallucinations or delusions. Both are real, treatable conditions that deserve compassion and support.

#MentalHealth #Support

Tardive Dyskinesia & Medication Awareness

Some medications can cause movement-related side effects, such as tardive dyskinesia. Learning about side effects and talking openly with healthcare providers is an important part of mental health care.

#MedicationAwareness #Advocacy

Mental Health Awareness

Mental health is part of everyone’s life. Awareness means reducing stigma, listening without judgment, and encouraging people to seek help when they need it.

#EndTheStigma #YouAreNotAlone
